Weekly recap (6 November to 8 November 2026): 53 % of the most likely outcomes happened

In this first week since the launch on 6 November, the most likely outcome announced by our AI happened in 88 of 166 matches (53 %), against 52 % announced on average. Announced and happened match closely, which is what matters for probabilities.

When our AI gave 65 % or more, the outcome happened in 25 of 27 matches, with 73 % announced on average. In the 60-70 % band it happened 86 % of the time (65 % announced), and in the 70-100 % band 94 % (77 % announced), so the AI was cautious there. The lower bands were close: 45 % announced and 44 % happened for 40-50 %, 53 % and 50 % for 50-60 %. The clearest case was Paris SG vs Troyes (87 % for a Paris SG win), which ended 1-0. The Süper Lig went 7/7.

The AI was clearly wrong on some matches. FK Partizan vs Macva (74 % for a Partizan win) ended 1-2, Club Brugge vs Anderlecht (67 %) ended 1-1, and the Bulgarian First League went 0/6, with the Liga Profesional at 2/7. These are only a few days of data, so expect noise. In the game where players face our AI, none of the 4 players beat it (3/5 for the AI, 2/5 for the best player).

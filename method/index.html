How we compute

For each match we mix two opinions, half and half:

  • Our own calculation: each team gets an attack and defence rating from its recent matches (the latest count more). From that we work out how many goals each side should score, then its chances to win, draw or lose.
  • The bookmakers' view: their odds with the margin removed. They already account for injuries and line-ups.

When there are no odds, only our calculation counts. As it is wrong more often on its own (47% hit rate instead of 55% with odds), its probabilities are then pulled 15% towards uncertainty, and the match is flagged ⚠️ no odds.

Our calculation also gives the expected number of goals, the most likely score, the chance of more than 2.5 goals and recent form.

The most likely matches

A match is highlighted when a team has at least a 65% chance to win. Every probability is locked 3 hours before kick-off, then checked against the real result. Nothing is deleted.

Our accuracy against the bookmakers

Two standard measures are used to grade probabilities: the Brier score and the log loss. For both, lower is better. Guessing at random (one third for each outcome) gives 0.667 and 1.099.

Our published probabilities are level with the bookmakers' market, the hardest benchmark to beat. Our model alone does slightly worse than the market: that is why we blend the two. These figures cover matches with odds; the sample is still small and gets sharper every day.

Why trust us?

We do not claim to beat the bookmakers: they are companies with huge resources. What we guarantee is that every number is published before the match and never changed afterwards. Anyone can check, and the results stay up even when they are bad.

What we do not promise

  • A probability is not a certainty: even at 80%, one outcome in five does not happen.
  • This is not betting advice.
  • The figures on this page are published as they are, even when they are bad.

Endpoints

Base URL : https://api.aifootpredictor.com/api/v1

GET /matchesA day's matches with 1 / X / 2 probabilities, most likely outcome, double chance, over 2.5 goals, both teams to score, clean sheets, the 5 most likely scores, expected goals, and the result once the match is over. Parameters: date (YYYY-MM-DD), competition, status (upcoming, finished).
GET /articlesOur AI-written pre-match articles, in English or French: title, lede and first paragraph, with the « Read the full analysis » link to show under it. Parameters: lang (en, fr), date, limit.
GET /tablesThe predicted end-of-season table (20,000 simulations every night): expected points, title, top 4 and relegation chances, attack and defence ratings. 8 leagues. Parameter: competition (E0, SP1, I1, D1, F1, P1, N1, T1).
GET /competitionsThe codes of the competitions covered, with the number of matches over 30 days.
GET /reliabilityOur reliability: Brier score and log loss on every finished match, compared with the market and with a random guess.

The rules

  • A visible, clickable link to aifootpredictor.com on every page that shows our data (the attribution block in every response is ready to display).
  • One key per account, tied to your site. Browser calls are only accepted from that domain.
  • 1,000 requests a day, reset at midnight (UTC).
  • No reselling of the data.

FAQ

Is the API really free?

Yes. 1,000 requests a day, no credit card. In return, a visible link to aifootpredictor.com on the pages that show our data.

How is the link checked?

Our bot reads the page you entered and looks for a link to aifootpredictor.com in its HTML. It checks when you create the key, when you click « Check my link », then once a day. The link must be in the HTML your server sends, not only added later by JavaScript.

What happens without a link?

You have 3 days after creating the key to add it. After that, the key is blocked until the link is found. If the link disappears for 3 checks in a row, the key is blocked too.

Which competitions are covered?

29 competitions, including the Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, the Champions League, the Europa League and the Conference League.

Are the probabilities reliable?

You can check: every probability is locked before kickoff then compared with the result, and the /reliability endpoint gives our live scores. Our Method page explains it all.

What exactly is in the API?

Only our own calculations and writing: probabilities, expected goals, most likely score, reliability, the final result of the match and excerpts of our pre-match articles (with the link to the full article). No odds, no logos, no raw provider data.

Frequently asked questions

Is the plugin free?

Yes. The block, the sidebar widget and the shortcode are free, with no signup and no key. Only the articles written for your site, your colours and your logo need the Pro plan.

Are Pro articles the same for every site?

No. Each preview is written for your site only, with its own angle and wording. No other client gets the same text, so there is no duplicate content in Google's eyes.

Can I review articles before they go live?

Yes: pick “Save as draft” in the settings. And if you edit an article by hand, the plugin never touches your text again.

Will the plugin slow my site down?

No. Widgets are lazy loaded in a separate frame, and articles are imported in the background, once an hour.

Which competitions are covered?

Around forty: the major European leagues, the Champions League, the Europa League, national cups, women's football and national teams.

Does it work with my theme and page builder?

Yes. The shortcode works anywhere WordPress accepts a shortcode: Elementor, Divi, Beaver Builder, the classic editor or blocks.

Is it betting advice?

No. These are statistical probabilities, for information only. 18+ only.
